# V3 + V4 Owned Challenger Decision Engine execution

This branch owns the V4 implementation of the governed owned-challenger decision-engine mission.

## Canonical V4 boundary

Extend the existing optimization / owned-vs-challenger capability. Do not revive the closed standalone comparator design as a second decision authority. Reuse canonical Official factual hydration, prediction, optimization, package optimizer, legality, price/sell-value, watchlist discovery, decision arbitration, report governance and serving contracts.

Required behavior:

- screen the complete authoritative owned 15 at every governed checkpoint;
- never hardcode Aina, Ødegaard, Rogers or any player as an OUT candidate;
- generate the challenger universe through governed DSS screening, not ownership, transfer trend, social hype or latest haul alone;
- permit zero, one or multiple `CHALLENGED_OWNED` players;
- expose inspectable owned rank, challenge pressure, replacement opportunity and confidence;
- expose pairwise evidence for every viable owned/challenger pair, including V4 3GW/5GW edge, strategic horizon, xMins/start, role, tactical matchup, rest/congestion, points routes, value, uncertainty, market evidence, structural gain, switching/hit cost, net projected gain and confidence;
- evaluate HOLD plus one/two/multi-transfer packages using the canonical package optimizer, respecting FT, hits, bank, purchase/sell value, max-three club, positional constraints and chip/Wildcard state;
- require robust net gain rather than positive raw xPts;
- retain V3 and V4 as independent model views. Never copy one engine into the other to manufacture parity;
- expose `ALIGN`, `DIVERGE`, `REVIEW_DIVERGENCE` or `NEUTRAL` with capability gaps and confidence;
- reduce/zero degraded, stale, blocked or NO-EQUIVALENT engine weight according to governance rather than silently substituting it;
- keep V4 non-executable whenever `execution_authorized=false`.

## Market timing and factual completeness

Use the shared canonical Official FPL predictor provider. FACT is Official current price, Official ownership and confirmed Official price change. MODEL is direction, progress, projection, trajectory, ETA and urgency. Threshold crossing is never confirmation. Confirm only after Official `now_cost` reconciliation. Every predictor datum carries freshness and stale predictor evidence cannot create urgency.

Natural Bahasa Indonesia ETA is required, for example `Memungkinkan kenaikan harga besok, 1 September 2026 sekitar pukul 06:00 WIB`, with the time derived from the governed Europe/London Official update cycle and timezone conversion, not hardcoded.

Before complete USER_REPORT serving, Owned Official FACT completeness must equal 15/15 and visible External DSS Watchlist Official FACT completeness 20/20, excluding owned and exactly 5 GK / 5 DEF / 5 MID / 5 FWD. `DATA_JOIN_DEFECT` remains internal-only. Deterministic reconciliation failure blocks publication rather than emitting partial Official rows.

## Football and structural explanation

Every material challenger decision must retain why the owned player is challenged, why the challenger is better, next-match implications, 3-5GW and 10-15GW outlook, xMins/role, tactical/system interaction, rest/congestion, routes to points, risks and flip conditions. Weather remains contextual and cannot independently trigger a transfer.

Generate `Main Transfer Battles` from engine output, not manually selected players. If no legal robust package qualifies, explicitly return `NO TRANSFER RECOMMENDED` with evidence.

## QA/QC acceptance

Acceptance requires all-15 dynamic screening, no player hardcode, DSS-generated challenger universe, deterministic pairwise matrix, independent V3/V4 views, 0/1/2/multi-transfer support, finance/legality constraints, predictor/ETA integration, lifecycle integration, 15/15 + 20/20 factual completeness, no `DATA_JOIN_DEFECT` leakage, no duplicate decision authority, no material runtime regression, tests GREEN, actual governed runtime + serving verification, and a report that explains WHY, WHEN, WHAT-IF and ACTION.

Audit RED for correctness/factual-integrity/authority/execution defects, YELLOW for design/maintainability/observability/operational risks, GREEN only after runtime verification.

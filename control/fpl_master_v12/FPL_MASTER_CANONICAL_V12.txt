9A. ROBUSTNESS / UNCERTAINTY DOMINANCE GATE
For close XI/bench/formation decisions and serious player/package comparisons, mean projected points alone is never sufficient.
A. Model the full availability/minutes mixture explicitly: P(available), P(start), bench-start state, P(cameo), P(DNP), late-cameo probability and the xMins distribution. Do not collapse these into one optimistic mean-minutes number.
B. AUTO-SUB BLOCKING COST is an explicit decision term. A DNP may preserve auto-sub value; a short cameo can destroy that option. Estimate the expected value of the bench alternative forfeited under cameo scenarios and include it in lineup utility/regret. Never treat DNP risk and cameo risk as equivalent.
C. Defensive-contribution/appearance floors are conditional on the minutes and role needed to earn them. Do not credit a nominal floor as if it were guaranteed when starter security or xMins is uncertain.
D. A small mean xPts edge that lies inside the model's uncertainty/credible decision band is NOT decisive. The uncertainty band must come from the current predictive distributions/calibration; never hardcode a fixed threshold such as 1.0 point from one historical case.
E. When mean utilities are close, compare lower-tail outcome, downside, expected regret, P(outperform), lineup optionality, auto-sub preservation and robustness to plausible lineup-news errors. Prefer the more robust structure unless the higher-upside route has enough evidence-adjusted edge to compensate for its extra downside.
F. Multi-GW role/starter-security evidence is a prior/shrinkage input. A one-GW projection with weak lineup confidence must not silently erase persistent evidence that a player is a weak link, rotation/cameo risk, or structurally fragile.
G. If the mean-xPts ranking and the robustness ranking disagree, surface both, explain the uncertainty driver, and classify the decision as ROBUSTNESS-DOMINATED / MEAN-EDGE-DOMINATED / INDETERMINATE rather than hiding the disagreement.
H. Post-match calibration updates P(start), xMins, cameo/DNP mixture and uncertainty calibration, but never creates player-specific permanent rules from one outcome. Learn the failure mode, not the hindsight winner.


9B. ONE-GW PUNT / RENTAL + ROLLING RE-OPTIMIZATION
A ONE-GW PUNT / RENTAL is a deliberate short-horizon transfer whose immediate value is concentrated in GW N and whose GW N+1 disposition must remain provisional until fresh re-optimization. It is NOT an ordinary 3–5GW transfer and NOT an automatic buy-then-sell script.

For every plausible one-GW route, evaluate against HOLD and normal multi-GW alternatives:
A. Exact GW N route: OUT -> PUNT-IN, with Gate0 legality, current price, FT/hit state where known, P(start), xMins distribution, tactical/role fit, team environment, opponent adjustment, set pieces/penalties, posterior GW N xPts distribution, P(outperform HOLD), floor/ceiling/tails and captaincy relevance where material.
B. Immediate net edge: show gross GW N edge and hit-adjusted edge separately. Never hide a hit inside the projection.
C. Cameo/rotation/downside: include late-cameo probability, auto-sub blocking where lineup related, injury/news risk, uncertainty band and expected regret.
D. Planned exit is a CONTEMPLATED route only. Record likely GW N+1 exit/reacquisition options, but never pre-commit the next transfer.
E. At GW N+1, BEFORE executing any planned exit, perform a FRESH FULL-UNIVERSE RESCAN using new V6/Official facts and updated Bayesian posteriors. Recompare:
   1 HOLD the punt longer;
   2 sell to the best fresh full-universe candidate;
   3 reacquire the original player where legal/optimal;
   4 choose a different structural/package move;
   5 roll/save the FT if waiting has higher utility.
F. Compute rolling horizon economics:
   - GW N immediate net;
   - GW N + GW N+1 two-GW net;
   - 3GW net;
   - 5GW net;
   - consumed FT now;
   - expected/possible FT required next GW;
   - dynamic FT shadow/opportunity value;
   - possible current or future hit;
   - purchase/sell/buy-back effects;
   - sell-value loss and affordability path;
   - route survival under price changes.
G. The FT shadow value is dynamic from the current 3–5GW universe frontier and squad needs. Never hardcode a universal fixed point value for one FT.
H. Define ABORT / KEEP-LONGER / EXIT triggers from fresh evidence: lineup security, role, injuries, price, competing urgent transfers, improved universe candidate, or changed fixture/team environment.
I. A one-GW punt may be recommended only when its distributional immediate upside plus any captaincy value is sufficient to compensate for hit/FT cost, planned-exit uncertainty, downside and lost flexibility.
J. If exit legality/affordability is fragile, label PUNT EXIT NOT SECURE. If the next-GW rescan changes the optimal destination, follow the fresh optimization rather than the old planned exit.
K. Visible serious punt comparison should include named routes and, where supportable:
   OUT -> PUNT-IN -> PROVISIONAL NEXT ROUTE;
   GW N EDGE;
   P(outperform HOLD);
   2GW NET;
   3GW NET;
   5GW NET;
   FT/HIT/PRICE COST;
